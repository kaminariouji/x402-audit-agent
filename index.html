How a call is paid

Request the resource with no payment. The 402 body carries accepts[] — scheme, network, USDC contract, amount in atomic units, payTo, maxTimeoutSeconds. Sign it (EIP-3009 TransferWithAuthorization on Base, SPL token transfer on Solana) and retry with the payment header. v1 (X-PAYMENT) and v2 (PAYMENT-SIGNATURE) are both accepted, so a client that only speaks one still gets served.
